# Synthetic / physical gap

The registered residual study shows a measurable domain gap. Existing
synthetic true impacts have much larger and more compact residuals than their
synthetic negatives, while physical true tracks overlap selected false
winners. Synthetic impact generation is therefore too clean or too separable
for its ranking results to establish physical readiness.

The temporal physical pass adds a second gap measurement: persistence and
onset have useful exploratory signal on physical tracks, but the same signal
is not yet represented as a frozen synthetic benchmark. The next synthetic
revision should be driven by measured physical patch distributions (including
weak/partial impacts, nuisance texture, and imperfect persistence), not by
optimizing the ten physical shots.

No synthetic generator parameters were changed in this pass. This is
intentional: the physical positive library is too small to justify estimating
a new impact model, and no holdout-preserving intervention has yet been
defined. Existing development/holdout metrics remain historical and unchanged.
