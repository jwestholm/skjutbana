Skjutbana V2.15 - Paired Hole-AI Evidence
=========================================

DELTA BASE
----------
Install on top of V2.14 background-generalisation delta.

PURPOSE
-------
V2.15 does NOT grant live authority. It makes the mild-vs-standard Hole-AI
comparison experimentally honest, measures paired error overlap, and creates a
reusable candidate shadow evidence annotator.

IMPORTANT AUDIT FIX
-------------------
The original V2.14 sweep varied one seed per profile. That seed controlled both
model/training randomness and whole-session data split. Therefore V2.14 sweep
non-holdout selection scores were not a fully paired apples-to-apples test.
Strict novel-background and real-hole holdouts remained excluded from training
and are still useful observations.

V2.15 separates split_seed from training/model seed and requires identical
session_assignment provenance before ensemble selection.
